FAQ

How do I connect my earbuds on Linux (Ubuntu / Zorin OS)?
First pair and connect your earbuds normally in your system Bluetooth settings. Then launch Xiaomi Earbuds — it detects connected Xiaomi/Redmi models in BlueZ, discovers the MIUI SPP RFCOMM channel dynamically, and completes SAFER+ authentication automatically.
Why does the app say "Connection refused" while earbuds are connected to my PC?
Xiaomi/Redmi earbuds only allow one host at a time on their MIUI SPP RFCOMM control channel (0xFD2D). If Dual-device connection is active and your smartphone (Xiaomi Earbuds app or HyperOS) is also connected, the phone may hold the control channel. Close the mobile app or briefly disconnect Bluetooth on the phone, then reconnect.
Why does Noise cancellation or Transparency revert to Off?
Xiaomi firmware enforces an in-ear wearing check before enabling ANC or Transparency. Place at least one earbud in your ear and try again (you can inspect live wearing state in Additional settings).
Why does the charging case battery show "—" when wearing both earbuds?
Xiaomi/Redmi charging cases do not have their own Bluetooth radio. Just like the official mobile app, the case percentage is read via the charging pins only while at least one earbud is seated in the open case. Once read, this app keeps showing the last reported case percentage for the rest of your session.
Why does single tap sometimes trigger by mistake?
Single-tap gestures on capacitive stems can trigger when adjusting the earbuds. Set "Tap once" to None in Gestures to avoid accidental pauses.
How does Immersive commute work on Linux?
It sends the hardware Immersive Commute command alongside Active Noise Cancellation and plays gentle ambient soundscapes (Airplane, Public transit, Train) through your desktop audio output.
Can I update earbud firmware from Linux?
No. OTA firmware flashing is intentionally disabled on Linux to avoid bricking risk. Use the official Xiaomi Earbuds app on Android or iOS for firmware upgrades.
